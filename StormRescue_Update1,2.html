Storm Rescue

Pull survivors from the open sea in 3D and bring them back to the harbor behind you. The storm grows with every rescue. The sea is endless: islands, rocks and ramps appear at random wherever you travel, and crashing into them damages your hull. Hit the yellow ramps at speed to launch your boat, but a hard landing hurts.

Design your own boat first (or pick a starred preset): more hull floats it, engines drive the boat, propellers and rotors let it fly, and low weight keeps it stable. You start as a little person on the dock: walk with W A S D (hold Shift to run) and touch your boat to climb in. Drive with W / S for throttle and A / D to steer (arrow keys work too). Hold Space to fly or climb, C to descend or dive (boats with a rotor can dive), and Shift to fire jets. Q / E or hold right-click to move the camera. Press Ctrl for shoulder camera lock: the mouse aims, and your character or boat turns to face where you look (A / D strafe on foot). X returns to the builder. Press Alt near an island or the harbor to step out and explore on foot (touch your boat to climb back in), and F to drop or raise an Anchor block. Press K in the builder to see and change every keybind.
